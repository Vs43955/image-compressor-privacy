Privacy Policy for Image Compressor

Last updated: September 2026

1. Overview

Image Compressor is an Android application that allows users to compress, resize, and process images on their device.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be processed when you use the application.

2. Images and Photos

Image Compressor allows you to select images from your device for compression and resizing.

Image processing is performed locally on your device. The application does not upload your selected images to a server operated by the developer for image processing.

If you choose to save an image to your device gallery or share an image with another application, that action is initiated by you.

3. Firebase Analytics

The application uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how features of the application are used and to improve the application.

Analytics information may include events such as application opens, image selection, compression activity, resizing activity, image saving, and image sharing.

Firebase Analytics may process information such as device and application information and usage data in accordance with Google's applicable policies.

4. Firebase Crashlytics

The application uses Firebase Crashlytics to identify crashes, errors, and stability issues.

Crashlytics may collect technical information about the application, device, operating system, and crash events to help diagnose and improve application stability.

5. Advertising

Image Compressor uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Advertising providers may process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, approximate location, application information, and advertising interaction data, depending on applicable settings and consent.

Where required, the application uses Google's consent mechanisms to provide users with choices regarding personalized advertising and the processing of personal data.

6. Privacy Choices and Consent

Depending on your location and applicable privacy regulations, you may be presented with options to consent to, manage, or decline certain uses of personal data for advertising and related purposes.

You may also be able to manage applicable privacy choices through the privacy options provided within the application.

7. Data Sharing

The application uses third-party services including Google Firebase and Google AdMob for analytics, crash reporting, and advertising.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

The developer does not sell users' personal information.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to use third-party services and application functionality in a manner intended to protect information processed through the application.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

Image Compressor is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through the application.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the application, services, or applicable requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the privacy practices of Image Compressor, please contact the developer through the developer's GitHub profile.
